What is BigDeal?

A passion for games, the drive to create something new and beautiful in the game world, staying true to our beliefs, and caring about every detail — that's what drives us at BigDeal. We see games as an art form, and we're good at it. We explore new mechanics, try things out, experiment; in short, we create with heart and give it everything we've got.

We believe that games help people discover and learn new things. We're sure that good, well-crafted games help you reset and shift your focus. And we believe that our games will leave a mark in players' hearts and make this world a little brighter!
